Mordred

This installs Mordred for you, the person logged in now: its own Hermes, the local Bonsai models, the keys and the encryption, and the two servers that start when you log in.

  • You will not type anything. When macOS asks, confirm with Touch ID.
  • Bonsai models already in ~/models/bonsai/models are used as they are. Otherwise the small model (about 1 GB) is downloaded, and you are asked before the large one (about 8 GB).
  • A backup file is saved to your Downloads folder. That file alone restores your keys on another Mac: keep a copy somewhere safe and private.

It takes a few minutes; the installer shows "Running package scripts" meanwhile.
